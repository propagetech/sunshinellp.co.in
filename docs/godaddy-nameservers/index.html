Change one setting in GoDaddy

Your new website will run on Cloudflare, a fast and secure service used by millions of websites. To connect it, one setting called nameservers must change in your GoDaddy account, where you bought the domain sunshinellp.co.in.

It takes about 5 minutes. Your email keeps working: we have already copied all your email settings to Cloudflare.

The two new nameservers

You will type (or copy) these two lines into GoDaddy. Please check every letter.

Nameserver 1celeste.ns.cloudflare.com
Nameserver 2corey.ns.cloudflare.com

They replace the two old GoDaddy nameservers:

BoxOld (remove)New (enter)
Nameserver 1ns33.domaincontrol.comceleste.ns.cloudflare.com
Nameserver 2ns34.domaincontrol.comcorey.ns.cloudflare.com
Steps

Change the nameservers

Best on a computer. Keep your phone nearby: GoDaddy may send a sign in code by SMS or email.
  1. Sign in to GoDaddy with the account you used to buy sunshinellp.co.in.
    Open GoDaddy domains
  2. You will see your list of domains (Domain Portfolio or My Products). Click sunshinellp.co.in.
  3. Click the DNS tab, then the Nameservers tab.
  4. Click Change Nameservers.
  5. Choose I'll use my own nameservers.
  6. In the first box, delete the old text and enter celeste.ns.cloudflare.com. In the second box, enter corey.ns.cloudflare.com. If there are more than two boxes, remove the extra ones.
  7. Click Save.
  8. GoDaddy shows a warning that your website and email may be affected. This warning is normal: we have already copied your settings. Tick the box to confirm and click Continue.
  9. The Nameservers tab now shows the two Cloudflare names. Take a screenshot and send it to us on WhatsApp.

That is all. You do not need to change anything else in GoDaddy.

What happens next

  • The change spreads across the internet in a few hours, at most 24 to 48 hours. During this time your current website and email keep working.
  • Cloudflare tells us when it is active. We then switch the website to the new one and check everything.
  • We will send you a message when the new website is live on www.sunshinellp.co.in.

Please avoid these

  • Do not cancel or transfer the domain. It stays with GoDaddy, and you keep renewing it at GoDaddy every year. Please keep auto renew on.
  • Do not cancel your GoDaddy website plan yet. The old website stays online until we tell you the new one is live.
  • Do not change your Zoho email settings. They do not need any change.
  • If GoDaddy asks about DNSSEC, leave it off.
  • Do not share your GoDaddy password with anyone, including us.

Prefer that we do it for you?

GoDaddy lets you give us access without sharing your password. It is called Delegate Access.

  1. In GoDaddy, click your name at the top right, then Account Settings.
  2. Open Delegate Access and click Invite to Access.
  3. Enter the name and email we send you on WhatsApp, choose Products & Domains, and send the invite.

You can remove our access at any time from the same page.
