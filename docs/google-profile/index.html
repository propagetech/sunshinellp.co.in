Part 1 · Today

Tell Google you are open

About 2 minutes. No ownership needed. Use any phone or computer signed in to a Google (Gmail) account.
  1. Open Google Maps and search for Sunshine Mining & Crushing Solutions LLP Hosur.
    Open Google Maps
  2. Tap your company name to open the listing (it has the red Permanently closed label).
  3. Tap Suggest an edit.
  4. Tap Operating status changed, then Reopen, then Submit.
  5. Go back and tap Suggest an edit again, then Change name or other details. Change the address to the new one below and tap Submit.
New addressPlot No. 54, HIG II, Brindhavan Nagar, Phase 7 TNHB, Bagalur Road, Hosur, Krishnagiri District, Tamil Nadu 635109

Good to know: Google checks every suggestion before it shows. It can take a few hours or a few days. If it still says closed after 3 days, do not worry: Part 3 fixes it directly once you own the listing.

Part 2 · This week

Take ownership of the listing

About 10 minutes, then Google verifies you (often a short video). Best on a computer, or a phone with the Google Maps app.

Which Google account to use

Use one account that the company will always keep, for example svgtempl@gmail.com. Please do not use a staff member's personal account or an agency's account. You stay the owner. If you would like our help later, you can add us as a Manager, and remove us at any time.

Steps

  1. Sign in to that Google account, then search Google for Sunshine Mining & Crushing Solutions LLP Hosur.
  2. On the right side (on a phone, scroll down), tap Own this business?
  3. Google now shows one of two screens:
    • "Manage now" or "Claim": no one owns it. Follow the screens. Go to step 4.
    • "Someone else may manage this Business Profile": someone else claimed it earlier (an old employee or website company, for example). Tap Request access, fill the short form and tap Submit. See "If someone else owns it" below.
  4. Google asks you to verify that you run the business. It picks the method. The most common one is a short video recorded on your phone in one go. Please keep these ready:
    • The company name board outside the office at Plot No. 54
    • The street or a nearby landmark, so Google can see the location
    • Inside the office, and any work equipment or vehicles with the company name
    • A business document, such as the GST certificate or LLP certificate, or a utility bill with the company name
    Sometimes Google offers a phone call, SMS or email code instead, which is quicker.
  5. Google reviews the video, usually within a few days, and emails you when you are verified.

If someone else owns it

  • The current owner gets an email from Google and has 3 days to reply.
  • If they approve, you can manage the listing straight away.
  • If they do not reply in 3 days, open Google's confirmation email, tap View request, then Verify, and follow the steps. Google does not always offer this.
  • If they refuse, or nothing is offered, send us a WhatsApp message. We will help you contact Google Business Profile support.

Part 3 · After you are verified

Remove "Permanently closed" and correct your details

About 5 minutes. These changes come straight from the owner, so Google applies them faster than suggestions.
  1. Search Google for your company name while signed in. Your listing opens with an Edit profile button. Tap it.
  2. Tap Hours. Next to Hours, tap the pencil (Edit), then choose Open with main hours. Tick each day you work and set the times, then tap Save. The Permanently closed label goes away.
  3. Tap Location and change the address to the new one. Check that the map pin sits on your office and tap Save. Google may ask you to verify again because the address changed. That is normal.
  4. Check the phone number and website. Use exactly these:

Please avoid these

  • Do not create a second listing before you try to claim this one. Two listings confuse Google and customers.
  • Do not change the company name (for example, adding "best crusher Hosur"). Google can suspend the listing.
  • Do not buy reviews or ask staff to write them. Ask real customers instead.
  • Do not share your Google password with anyone, including us. Use the Manager option to give access.

If Google will not change the address on the old listing, it may ask you to make a new listing at Plot No. 54. If that happens, tell us first and we will guide you through it.
