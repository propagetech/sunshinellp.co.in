Connect your website to Google

Google Search Console is a free Google service. It tells Google about your new website pages, warns you if Google finds a problem, and shows what people type into Google to find you.

It takes about 10 minutes, in two short sittings. You do a few clicks, we do the technical part in between.

Before you start

Please do this only after we tell you your new website is live on www.sunshinellp.co.in. One step needs the new setup to be working.

  • Use a computer if possible.
  • Use the same Google account as your Google listing: svgtempl@gmail.com. Then the company owns both, in one place.
  • You stay the owner. We only get access to help, and you can remove it at any time.
Part 1 · About 5 minutes

Add your website

  1. Open Google Search Console and sign in with svgtempl@gmail.com.
    Open Search Console
  2. If it is your first time, Google shows a Select property type screen straight away. Otherwise, click the box at the top left and choose Add property.
  3. On the left side, under Domain, type the address below (no www, no https) and click Continue.
    Domainsunshinellp.co.in
  4. Google shows a box called Verify domain ownership via DNS record, with a long line of text that starts with google-site-verification=. Click Copy next to it.

    If Google offers to connect to Cloudflare or another provider for you, do not use that option. Choose the option to copy the record instead. We manage that part for you.

  5. Paste that line into a WhatsApp message to us and send it. It is not a password and is safe to share.
  6. You can now close the page. Google remembers where you stopped.

Our part: we add that line to your domain settings, usually within an hour of your message, and tell you on WhatsApp when it is done.

Part 2 · After our message, about 5 minutes

Confirm, then give us access

  1. Open Search Console again with svgtempl@gmail.com.
    Open Search Console
  2. Google shows sunshinellp.co.in as not verified. Click it, then click Verify.
  3. You should see Ownership verified. Click Go to property.

    If Google says it could not find the record, wait one hour and click Verify again. Changes can take a little while to reach Google.

  4. In the menu on the left, scroll down and click Settings, then Users and permissions.
  5. Click Add user. Enter the email address we send you on WhatsApp, set Permission to Full, and click Add.

That is all from your side. We then send Google the list of your website pages (the sitemap) and check that Google has picked up the new pages in place of the old ones.

What happens next

  • Google starts reading the new pages within a few days. The full picture in Search Console builds up over two to four weeks.
  • Google may send emails to svgtempl@gmail.com, for example a welcome email or a monthly summary. They are normal and safe to read. If one says there is a problem, forward it to us.
  • There is nothing to renew and nothing to pay.

Please avoid these

  • Do not use Removals or Remove property. They can hide your website from Google.
  • Do not give Owner permission to anyone. Full is enough for us.
  • Do not share your Google password with anyone, including us.

Prefer that we do it for you?

We can set it up from our side and then add svgtempl@gmail.com as an owner, so the company still owns it. You will get an email from Google; open it and you are done. Just tell us on WhatsApp.
