What we do

Eight services across the mining life cycle

From running your crusher plant to supplying graded aggregate and securing statutory approvals, here is the full range of work that keeps a mining and crushing operation moving.

Operation & maintenance

Operation and Maintenance Contractors

Sunshine is an established provider of operation and maintenance (O&M) for crushing plants, and we have expanded that into aggregates and the production of M.Sand and plastering sand.

We perform thorough analysis and research on the development and implementation of O&M, and deliver quality, reliable service as an integral part of your operation, keeping output steady and predictable.

Mining

Raising Contractors in Mining

Our multidisciplinary engineering and technical services team offers practical, innovative and reliable designs, with the ability to provide a total mine package.

Our 2017 vision still holds today: to offer a high level of service to the mining industry that exceeds current standards. Services include mine contracting, raise mining, mine development, engineering and a variety of specialty services, backed by regulatory knowledge and regional expertise.

Trading & supply

Trading: M.Sand, P.Sand and Aggregate Supply

We trade, distribute and supply quality concrete sand (M.Sand) and plastering sand (P.Sand), plus aggregate in 40, 20, 12 and 6 mm sizes, quarry dust and crushed rock fines, wet mix macadam (WMM) and granular sub base (GSB).

  • Material delivered on the committed timeline, with 24x7 service
  • Right quality and right quantity, our motto, with GPS tracking
  • Serving premium developers and builders across the Bangalore and Hosur market

Compliance

All Statutory Permissions for Mines and Crushers

We cover the permissions and liaison work that keeps a mine or crusher compliant, from exploration and plant design through to closure, helping reduce risk and keep projects moving.

We specialise in:

  • Identification and assessment of mineral prospects
  • Liaison with government, public and private sector organisations
  • Applications for allocation of a mineral block through the Ministry of Coal, Government of India
  • Proposals for mineral concessions (RP, PL and ML)
  • Proposals for land acquisitions under the CBA Act, 1957 and the LA Act
  • CSR and EHS studies

PWD approval

M.Sand and P.Sand PWD Approval and Site Lab

Under the new M.Sand policy released by the Tamil Nadu government on 09.03.2023, it is compulsory to obtain M.Sand and P.Sand product approval from the Public Works Department (PWD) to produce M.Sand and concrete sand, and a testing laboratory at the work site is mandatory.

We secure that PWD approval and set up the on-site lab facilities for you, in a cost-effective manner.
