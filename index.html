About Sunshine

One solution provider, across the entire mining life cycle

Established in 2017 by four promoters with specialised skills in planning, mining and crushing, logistics, statutory fulfilment and accounting, Sunshine Mining & Crushing Solutions LLP is a concept-to-closure partner for mining and aggregate projects.

Whatever the mine and whatever the mineral, we deliver quality services across every phase: front-end geology, processing, quality and environmental work, conceptual and detailed design, project management, construction, operations and mine closure. That continuity keeps knowledge and accountability in one place.

Eight services, one accountable team

Operation & Maintenance Contractors

Raising Contractors in Mining

M.Sand & Aggregate Supply
