About Sunshine

One accountable partner, concept to closure

A Hosur mining, crushing and aggregate company built on safety, quality and integrity, supporting the whole project life cycle so knowledge and accountability stay in one place.

Who we are

Established in 2017, by people who know the work

Sunshine Mining & Crushing Solutions LLP was established in 2017 by four promoters with specialised skills in planning, mining and crushing, logistics, statutory fulfilment and accounting. Together they make Sunshine a complete solution provider for mining and aggregate needs.

Whatever the mine and whatever the mineral, we deliver quality services across the entire life cycle: front-end geology, processing, quality and environmental consultation, conceptual and detailed design, project management, construction, operations and mine closure. That concept-to-closure capability provides continuity of support and knowledge across every phase.

We nurture ethical business values and develop our people to build and sustain newer business models for greater community development.

What we bring

Specialist expertise across the mining project

In the early stages we use geology, mineral resource estimation, mine planning and metallurgical studies to add value: linking resource estimates to the right mining method, designing efficient flow sheets, and reducing plant footprint and capital cost.

  • Front-end services: exploration assistance, resource and reserve estimates, mine planning and design, geotechnical and environmental services, and scoping, pre-feasibility and feasibility studies
  • Project, logistics and construction management
  • EPCM, EPC and Project Management Contractor (PMC) delivery
  • Annual maintenance contracts for crusher plants producing M.Sand and aggregates
  • Plant start-up, commissioning and crusher plant erection with finalised flow sheets
  • Material trading (aggregates, M.Sand and P.Sand) and logistics arrangement
  • Mine closure and rehabilitation
