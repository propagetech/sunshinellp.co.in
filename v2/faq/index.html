Frequently asked questions

Straight answers on materials, delivery, plant O&M, statutory permissions and PWD approval under the 2023 M.Sand policy.

Supply and delivery

What materials and aggregate sizes do you supply?

We supply manufactured sand (M.Sand) and plastering sand (P.Sand), aggregate in 40, 20, 12 and 6 mm sizes, quarry dust and crushed rock fines, wet mix macadam (WMM) and granular sub base (GSB). Right quality and right quantity is our motto.

What is the difference between M.Sand and P.Sand?

M.Sand is manufactured sand used as concrete sand. P.Sand is plastering sand, used for plastering work. We trade, distribute and supply both, along with graded aggregate, so you can source concrete sand, plastering sand and aggregate from one supplier.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Hosur, Tamil Nadu and primarily serve the Bangalore and Hosur construction market, supplying premium developers and builders. Our mining and crushing services are delivered across India.

Do you track deliveries?

Yes. We deliver material on the committed timeline with 24x7 service, and every delivery is GPS tracked so you can see the right quantity arriving at the right place.

Plants and operations

Can you run and maintain our existing crusher plant?

Yes. We provide end-to-end operation and maintenance (O&M) for crusher plants producing aggregates, M.Sand and plastering sand. We analyse your plant, implement disciplined O&M, and keep output reliable and predictable.

Which crusher plant brands do you erect and audit?

We deliver turnkey erection and plant audits for brands including Puzzolana, Propel, Metso and Helstone, plus modular and fully custom plant options. We cover design, supply, installation, commissioning and after-sales service.

Can you supply manpower for a crusher plant or mine?

Yes. Our manpower services cover geological exploration, geotechnical services and mine planning, electrical installation and maintenance, mechanical work on high-capacity mining machinery, and civil and infrastructure planning, along with equipment selection and manpower planning.

Do you take on accounting job work?

Yes. Our accounting job work covers book-keeping, preparing and filing taxes, audits, vendor payments and client billing, profit and loss statements, monthly financial statements, bank reconciliation, and payroll and withholding taxes.

Permissions and PWD approval

Do you help with PWD approval for M.Sand and P.Sand?

Yes. Under the Tamil Nadu M.Sand policy released in March 2023, PWD product approval is compulsory to produce M.Sand and concrete sand, and an on-site testing laboratory is mandatory. We secure that PWD approval and set up the site lab for you in a cost-effective manner.

Which statutory permissions can you handle for a mine or crusher?

We identify and assess mineral prospects, liaise with government, public and private sector organisations, apply for allocation of mineral blocks through the Ministry of Coal, prepare proposals for mineral concessions (RP, PL and ML) and for land acquisition under the CBA Act, 1957 and the LA Act, and carry out CSR and EHS studies.
