PWD approval
08

M.Sand and P.Sand PWD Approval and Site Lab

Under the M.Sand policy released by the Tamil Nadu government in March 2023, it is compulsory to obtain M.Sand and P.Sand product approval from the Public Works Department (PWD) to produce M.Sand and concrete sand, and a testing laboratory at the work site is mandatory.

  • We secure the PWD product approval for M.Sand and P.Sand
  • We set up the on-site testing laboratory
  • Both in a cost-effective manner
