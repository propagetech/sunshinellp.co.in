About Sunshine

One accountable partner, concept to closure

A Hosur-based mining, crushing and aggregate company built on safety, quality and integrity, supporting the whole project life cycle so knowledge and accountability stay in one place.

Who we are

Working since 2017, by people who know the work

Sunshine has been working since 2017, set up by four promoters with specialised skills in planning, mining and crushing, logistics, statutory fulfilment and accounting, and was registered as Sunshine Mining & Crushing Solutions LLP in 2019. Together they make Sunshine a complete solution provider for mining and aggregate needs.

We nurture ethical business values and develop our people to build and sustain newer business models for greater community development. Whatever the mine and whatever the mineral, we deliver quality services across the entire life cycle: front-end geology, processing, quality and environmental consultation, conceptual and detailed design, project management, construction, operations and mine closure. That concept-to-closure capability provides continuity of support and knowledge synergy across every phase.

What we bring

Specialist expertise across the mining project

We are recognised in mining, crushing and the trading of M.Sand and plastering sand, with experience in every facet of mining project assessment, development and optimisation. In the early stages we use geology, mineral resource estimation, mine planning and metallurgical studies to add real value: optimising the link between resource estimates and the chosen mining method, increasing net present value, designing efficient flow sheets, and reducing plant footprint and capital cost.

Our areas of expertise include:

  • Front-end services: exploration assistance, resource and reserve estimates, mine planning and design, geotechnical and environmental services, and scoping, pre-feasibility and feasibility studies
  • Project, logistics and construction management
  • EPCM, EPC and Project Management Contractor (PMC) delivery
  • Annual maintenance contracts for crusher plants producing M.Sand and aggregates
  • Plant start-up, commissioning and crusher plant erection with finalised flow sheets
  • Material trading (aggregates, M.Sand and P.Sand) and logistics arrangement
  • Mine closure and rehabilitation
